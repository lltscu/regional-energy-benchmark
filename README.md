# Regional Integrated Energy Test System: Compact Benchmark Parameters

This repository provides the anonymous parameter tables for *A Geospatially Constrained Benchmark for Joint Construction of Regional Integrated Energy Networks* by Lingtao Li, Yue Xiang, and Qinglai Guo.

## Current dataset

**Version:** 2026.11-compact  
**File:** [RIETS_Compact_Parameters_2026_11.xlsx](RIETS_Compact_Parameters_2026_11.xlsx)

The workbook describes a geographically constrained synthetic electricity-gas-cooling benchmark. It contains 37 statistical demand objects, 56 physical receiving zones, six substations with twelve main transformers, two independent gas handovers, four cooling plants, and 51 heat-exchange interfaces. Statistics, facilities, equipment, interfaces, and source networks are recorded separately. Supply and equipment settings are research configurations; the network is not an operating-asset inventory.

The 26 worksheets contain system definitions, demand allocations, equipment, hydraulic and installation parameters, selected nodes and branches, corridor arrangements, annual controlling conditions, and construction comparisons. All exported positions use a common local coordinate frame and anonymous object identifiers. Gas construction inputs and the separate inlet-pressure operating check are labelled individually. Numerical co-location connectors are distinguished from routed construction branches and registered internal circuits.
