- **Research configuration.** Demand intensities and profiles, synthetic supply configurations, service allocations, technical catalogues, installation rules, and cost parameters are defined for the benchmark. Their definitions and units are included in the workbook. They are distinct from observations of operating facilities.

All released positions use anonymous identifiers and a local coordinate frame. The transformation preserves the geometry used by the benchmark calculations; it is not a guarantee that the source region cannot be recognized.
